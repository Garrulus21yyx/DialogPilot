# Illustrative cases (not live business facts)

- “Find the priciest jacket upgrade, keeping size M. Just compare.” Filter to
  supported size-M variants, compare prices, report the choice and difference;
  do not prepare a purchase merely because a selection was found.
- “Use my gift card.” After a completed comparison, retain selected variants and
  their valid evidence. Resolve the payment information and continue preparation;
  repeat a catalog read only if specific necessary evidence is missing or stale.
- “Upgrade the speaker and lamp; leave the headphones alone.” Track two selected
  variants independently and preserve the excluded item. If one has no eligible
  replacement, report that limit without losing the successful comparison.
