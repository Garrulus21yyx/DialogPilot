## Current code connection

- `TargetTurnContextLoader.load` loads public messages/summary and optionally
  reusable knowledge evidence. It does not load prior business tool observations.
- `TargetTurnContext.observed_execution` is populated by same-turn observation
  continuation. A new user turn does not inherit the previous turn's result board
  merely because its public reply is in history.
- `TurnRuntime._assemble_response` sends a response-only candidate with no board
  to the common response boundary. `_response_context` derives business facts and
  receipts from that board, not from historical assistant prose.
- Publication commands and the private publication evidence column currently
  carry reusable `knowledge_evidence`, not general prior business observations.

This explains why a conversational follow-up can retain prices in public prose
but lose their original structured support at authoring/verification. It does not
authorize treating every historical sentence as a current fact. Prior observations
must remain scoped and timestamped; a new write still needs current prerequisites.
